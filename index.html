CoachSys

Co-founder & Full Stack Developer

need: a personal trainer wanted his own coaching method to work online.

An online coaching app built together with the trainer, around his method. Each week, athletes get their routine through a link valid only for that week and log their sets from their phone, with a rest timer and progress charts. The next routine starts from the previous one and the athlete’s notes, so the coach only adjusts what needs to change.

  • Rails 8
  • PostgreSQL
  • Hotwire
  • Tailwind
  • Docker
coachsys.pro
origin: own_need

FightPlan

Founder & sole developer

need: to understand how elite athletes think, and bring that into my own training.

Jiu-jitsu fight plans that map how elite athletes actually fight, so everyday practitioners can understand how those athletes think and carry that into their own training. Techniques are organized across the phases of a match — takedown, sweep, pass, progression, submission, escape — and linked to the exact moments they happen in fight footage, with counts of what repeats from fight to fight. Published as a static site, with no server or database.

  • Astro
  • TypeScript
  • Tailwind
fightplan.pro
origin: own_need

FocusPocus

Creator & sole developer

need: a simple, repeatable way to plan the week in focus blocks.

A weekly planner of focus blocks, built around two learning ideas: spaced repetition and interleaving. It’s timeless — a Monday-to-Sunday cycle that repeats, so nothing is ever overdue. Blocks are drag-and-drop, data stays in the browser and it works offline. Available in English, with a Brazilian Portuguese version at focuspocus.com.br.

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Tailwind
  • Vitest
focus.brunon.dev
origin: own_need

GainScore

Founder & sole developer

need: to follow my training set by set, control my rest times and actually see my progress.

A training tracker I built to follow my own workouts closely. Each set was logged from the phone, and a rest timer started on its own as soon as a set was done, kept the screen awake and chimed when it was time to go again. Charts showed how each exercise and body measurement evolved. And to skip retyping plans, you could paste one as text or upload a spreadsheet, a PDF or a photo, and Claude (Anthropic API) turned it into sessions and exercises in the background.

  • Rails 8
  • PostgreSQL
  • Hotwire
  • Claude API
